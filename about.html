About

We write the answer we'd want to find.

Ask Remi started from a simple frustration: most search results are optimized for search engines, not for the person reading them. Every post here is written to be useful first — sourced, specific, and short enough to actually finish.

Remi Grant writes and edits Ask Remi, with occasional guest explainers from specialists in money, tools, and internet policy. Every claim is checked against a primary source before it goes up, and every post is dated so you know how fresh the advice is.

Got a question you can't find a straight answer to? That's exactly what this site exists for.
